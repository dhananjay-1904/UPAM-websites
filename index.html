Why UPAM

A home away from home

Three simple commitments guide everything we organise.

I

Festivals, together

Diwali, Holi, Dussehra and more — celebrated the way our families back home would recognise.

II

Language & culture

Keeping Awadhi and Hindi alive for the next generation growing up in WA.

III

A support network

New to Perth? UPAM connects you with families who've walked the same path.
