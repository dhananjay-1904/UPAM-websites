Our story

Uttar Pradesh Awadh Manch WA (UPAM) was formed by families from Uttar Pradesh and the Awadh region who wanted to keep our traditions, language and sense of community alive here in Western Australia. What began as a handful of households sharing festivals together has grown into a wider community network across Perth.

Our mission

To create a welcoming home for Awadh and UP families in WA — organising festivals, cultural events and community support, and helping new arrivals settle in and find their people.
